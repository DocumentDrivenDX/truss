# CONTRACT-011: Support evidence receipt

**Version:** proposed truss-evidence/0.1.0. **Status:** draft.

## Scope and authority

A receipt records a bounded run and its actual outcomes. A support statement derives a claim only from trusted immutable receipts matching the complete requested profile. It cannot transfer Weft compiler or UMF parser evidence into Truss PostgreSQL execution qualification. Documentation/type/schema inspections have their own explicit evidence scope.

## Required receipt members

| Member | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| interfaceVersion, runId | Exact evidence version and unique immutable run identity |
| procedure | Executable command/script reference, byte digest and working-root context; no credentials |
| inputs | Contract/model/layout/binding/corpus/implementation exact version and SHA-256 pins, including uncommitted source snapshot when applicable |
| environment | Actual runtime/adapter/server patch/platform/isolation/journal/policy/pooler profile; unsupported dimensions explicitly absent |
| requiredCases | Immutable complete manifest of case IDs and their criterion/invariant coverage |
| outcomes | Exactly one result for every required case: passed, failed, skipped, blocked or not_run, with result/log artifact references |
| observedAt, duration | Qualified observation time and exact duration representation |
| trust | Runner identity and verification provenance; external receipt is not trusted by self-declaration |
| scope, limitations | Exact tested subset/layer and remaining assumptions |

References include content digests and are resolved through host-authorized local/artifact stores; input cannot load code, issue network requests or nominate a trusted runner. Unknown receipt version blocks certification. Duplicate IDs, missing outcomes, unexpected required-case substitution or unavailable result artifacts invalidate the receipt. Optional additional outcomes may be retained but cannot replace the required manifest.

## Derived support states

A requested version/subset with no matching complete trusted receipt is unverified. A matching run with failures reports failed; skipped/blocked/not_run required cases remain unverified or incomplete, never passed. Exact pin mismatch produces stale for the requested claim while retaining the old historical run. Timestamp recency alone cannot make mismatched evidence current.

Qualified means all required cases passed for the exact selected manifest/profile and artifacts are available/trusted. It is not a universal adapter/server/semantic guarantee. Any broad claim must enumerate the conjunction of its required independent receipts, including native/bypass/browser/host/interchange evidence as appropriate. Two implementations passing shared fixtures cannot establish independent interchange or native oracle correctness unless those runs exist.

## Reproducibility and immutability

### Installed-profile evidence for database claims

A receipt used for database-enforcement qualification must pin a canonical installed guard/privilege inventory as an input artifact, in addition to layout SQL. Capture both the actual ordinary-writer identity and the administrative observer used to verify protected state; record their different responsibilities without credentials. The inventory covers CONTRACT-004's grants, callable functions/owners/search paths, RLS flags, policies, enabled triggers and relevant bypass capabilities. Its extraction procedure, normalization version and raw observations are receipt artifacts. OIDs and ordering may be normalized only by the declared inventory profile; privilege distinctions and function/policy meaning cannot be omitted.

Each advertised assertion maps to the required bypass cases and their tested writer paths in requiredCases. A receipt whose manifest tests only marker-table uniqueness cannot certify unavoidable canonical key derivation. A SQL file hash alone cannot establish the installed privilege inventory, and a self-reported role label cannot establish actual test execution context.

Current support verification compares installed inventory with the pinned qualified inventory through a trusted nonmutating observation. Record its time, context and result separately from the immutable test run. A match supports the observed installation at that point; it does not promise guards remain unchanged indefinitely. Drift or inability to observe required surfaces yields stale/unverified qualification. Deployments claiming continuing qualification must control administrative changes and refresh verification after any guard/grant change. Ordinary mutation calls cannot silently acquire elevated permissions to perform that verification.

### Proposed installed-policy observation binding

Complete-feed qualification additionally inventories transaction/member/prerequisite stores and every producer/finalizer/retention helper under CONTRACT-006. Required bypass cases attempt caller-selected producing xid/epoch, altered original bytes, forged finalized generation, direct store mutation and partition-child access. Record the actual ordinary writer and independent observer; a privileged finalizer fixture alone cannot prove unavoidable registration. Coherent observation covers both canonical writer entry paths and store helpers so an unguarded alternate path cannot retain a completeness claim.

[Installed-policy binding](bindings/truss-installed-policy-v0.1.d.ts) enumerates resolved writer/observer roles, inherited and role-switch capabilities, effective relation/column grants, partition parents, RLS policy expressions, triggers and their exact callable identity, constraint definitions, routine ownership/definer/configuration/body artifacts, sequence rights and administrative bypass evidence. Qualified routine identity includes native argument identity so an overloaded name cannot substitute for the tested function. A missing function search-path setting remains distinguishable from an explicitly pinned setting. Preserve raw native observations alongside the extraction procedure and normalized inventory.

The extraction profile must declare its complete namespace/dependency closure before capture: canonical and derived relations, all partitions, protected markers/receipts, reachable routines and dependent guards, writer role closure, PUBLIC rights, schemas and any relevant host extension path. Empty lists cannot establish that there are no required guards; compare to the independently selected physical/profile inventory. Effective privileges are evaluated for every authorized writer path, including role changes, rather than interpreting one ACL row as complete access. Resolve ownership/role/schema manipulation, session replication and guard-alteration capabilities explicitly. Default privileges and schema creation rights require capture when the qualified profile permits object creation; otherwise that profile must prohibit that path and prove the prohibition. Unsupported native catalog dimensions make capture unavailable, not an empty successful inventory.

Normalization may sort unordered inventories and replace volatile native identifiers with qualified identities only when the pinned procedure declares how ambiguity is detected. Preserve definitions, role distinctions, policy composition, trigger mode and partition membership; do not whitespace-rewrite executable bodies as if semantic equivalence were proven. Verify artifact bytes/digests. Unknown required fields, name collisions, incomplete closure or unresolved dependency identity block qualification.

An observation returns match, drift or unavailable. Match requires exact qualified inventory/profile equality and a coherent capture under the qualified administrative change-control protocol. If policy or role changes can race the multi-query capture and coherence cannot be established, return unstable_observation. A pre/post hash alone is not evidence that an intervening change was impossible. Capture database target and actual session/current roles independently; caller-supplied role strings are not authority. The observation is a point-in-time result and cannot authorize future mutation or confer elevated observer powers on the writer. Exact catalog query bundle, version-specific role semantics, JSON wire schema and administrative serialization remain review outputs; the declaration is no native qualification claim.

### Raw role-graph observation proposal

The [PostgreSQL 17 role-graph query](installed-role-graph-v0.1.proposal.sql) captures explicit role attributes and each native membership's own ID, role/member/grantor IDs and independent admin/inherit/set options. PostgreSQL's [pg_auth_members catalog](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/catalog-pg-auth-members.html) distinguishes these options; [pg_roles](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/view-pg-roles.html) exposes role attributes. Neither membership reachability alone nor one role's inherit flag establishes effective privileges or SET ROLE capability. Preserve raw memberships separately, including distinct grantors; no name-only deduplication or option merging may erase the original observation.

This candidate captures the complete row inventory for its selected role/membership projections within one statement, using capped native-ID-ordered prefixes solely to detect overflow. More than 10,000 roles or 100,000 memberships invalidates the whole observation. The extra row is charged and retained privately; a prefix must never become a complete role closure. Both arrays and overflow flags belong to the same successful statement result. No password, arbitrary role configuration, effective ACL, object policy, executable routine body or writer-context observation is supplied. Those remain separate installed-inventory inputs. Capturing these private role facts is not authority to disclose them to an application caller.

Admit the selected private administrative observer, actual PostgreSQL 17 build/profile and original database/cluster registry before dispatch. Native role OIDs are canonical text correlation handles within this original observation, not globally stable role identities or authorization tokens. Resolve every role/member/grantor handle against the complete observed role inventory; duplicate OIDs, missing references or unexpected catalog fields/types make the result unavailable. Preserve exact role names, options and privileged flags. The observed session/current/role-setting fields describe this observer operation; they cannot stand in for the independently captured writer or the original acting caller inside a protected definer path. No SET ROLE or session mutation is performed by the query.

Proposed observation limits additionally require one 32 MiB encoded text carrier, JSON depth 16, 1,500,000 parser nodes, 256 MiB peak owned bytes, 1 GiB cumulative work bytes, 30,000 ms operation and 5,000 ms containment. They are independent unadopted limits, including overflow rows, framing, raw/native/parser/tree/correlation/output copies and failed work. Maximum row counts do not promise that every combination fits the other ceilings. Apply bounded transport/parser admission before materialization; native JSON aggregation, scan/sort/intermediate memory and server execution remain independently qualified. No LIMIT or post hoc text-length check certifies native work or maximum allocation. Exhaustion, missing native termination or unknown containment cannot yield a partial graph or a match receipt.

Build the complete native-OID-to-exact-name table first, refusing duplicate OIDs or name collisions. Resolve every membership's role/member/grantor through that same observation. Do not resolve one handle by querying a later catalog snapshot. Refuse ambiguous repeated normalized member/role/grantor identity rather than merging it. Keep an original native-row-to-normalized-entry correspondence under private extraction custody; no raw row may disappear or contribute twice.

For this proposed role projection, order roles by unsigned UTF-8 bytes of their exact names, then each member's memberships by granted-role bytes followed by grantor bytes. Do not case-fold, Unicode-normalize, locale-sort, sort numeric OID text lexically or OR options across grants. Original native row order/OIDs remain evidence, but do not define cross-installation semantic equality. Canonical serialization follows the already selected inventory/basis profile; an unregistered serializer cannot produce a qualified digest merely by sorting arrays. Preserve all selected role capability flags in their governing normalized role/administrative-capability surfaces, with original evidence for each derived capability.

BootstrapInventoryBasis already includes policyMeaning derived from InstalledPolicyInventory; its memberships therefore retain the new grantor field without a second bootstrap policy schema. Compare exact role/member/grantor/options and every selected role/capability fact in that existing meaning projection. Changed grantor with otherwise equal effective options is declared drift under this proposed exact provenance-sensitive profile. Reassigned native OIDs with identical resolved names and facts alone are not semantic drift, provided complete original correspondence and observer/administrative coherence still pass. Native OID equality alone cannot establish an unchanged meaning. Raw extraction/observer context remains separately mandatory even though the existing stable basis excludes its volatile custody facts.

This projection does not compute effective privileges, authenticated login feasibility, inherited/SET paths or the absence of administrative bypass. Those independent native evaluations remain required before match/qualification. A complete raw graph and a valid normalized tree cannot discharge an assertion whose writer paths or object/routine dependencies remain unobserved. All comparisons remain under the existing installed-inventory/current-authority protocol; no new authorization token or singleton is introduced.

The graph seeds complete role-path and administrative-capability evaluation under the selected installed-policy profile; it does not itself evaluate them. Effective relation/column/routine/sequence/schema rights still require exact native evaluation for every admitted writer path, including PUBLIC, ownership, inheritance, allowed role changes and administrative bypass. The profile must reconcile individual grantor observations with its normalized identity/equality rules and retain original raw provenance; a normalized member list cannot certify the absence of omitted grant paths. Unsupported major versions or catalogs refuse rather than guessing absent options.

A statement snapshot makes these role facts mutually coherent, not the full multi-query installed inventory. Use the existing administrative change-control/current-authority protocol to bind this graph to the separately captured object/policy/routine/privilege facts and writer contexts. An ABA role/grant change between captures cannot be excluded by equal pre/post hashes alone. Missing cluster-wide role-change serialization, unresolved roots or incomplete dependency closure yields unstable_observation/incomplete_scope as already defined, not match. Native compilation, actual observer privilege/identity, normalization, effective-path evaluation, finite allocation/work and administrative coherence remain adoption outputs.

The [separate source receipt](../../04-build/evidence/design-audit/installed-role-graph-source.json) records owner parser/source archive/JSON reload/stable export for this one SELECT. Partial DDL extraction leaves it unhandled and complete=false. This evidence is separate from the seventeen-draft round-trip receipt; it does not increment that receipt's tested inventory or establish any native/catalog/enforcement claim.

Grantee zero becomes the tagged public branch; other grantees become tagged role names, including a real role literally named public. Never derive grantor from owner or observer. Bootstrap policyMeaning inherits the required grants inventory without a second independent projection. Original-role disappearance, ambiguous identity, inconsistent repeated headers, mixed no-grant sentinel/real-grant rows, unsupported privilege, incomplete scope or resource truncation prevents comparison. Native implementation and producer adoption remain unfinished.

This unadopted binding rejects older projections missing namespace meaning; no deployed wire compatibility or native support is claimed.

Published receipts are immutable; a rerun creates a new runId and new content digest, linked to the same claim with new pins. Never edit old failures into successes. Redact secrets at capture rather than rewriting hashed logs later without a new artifact identity.

Available requires a trusted qualification receipt covering that complete tuple and capability subset, plus a coherent target-inventory observation for the named database/schema. Installed capabilities additionally pin installationId; fresh-namespace bootstrap instead pins its namespace observation and cannot claim an installed layout. Validate receipt provenance, required cases and native target compatibility under this contract; matching digest syntax or a successful connection is insufficient. A different installation or a restrictive qualified subset cannot borrow availability from another tuple. Missing qualifications remain unverified or unavailable, never wildcard support.

The profile determines required inventory surfaces and observation protocol per family: bootstrap readiness means the adapter/admin procedure can attempt a qualified fresh-namespace installation, not that a database is already installed; mutation readiness needs installed write guards; feed readiness additionally needs producer/manifest/retention prerequisites; Weft execution readiness needs registered backend and obligation discharge. Thus not_installed can be appropriate for mutation while bootstrap is available. Validate selection against its family's exact profile rather than imposing one universal installed-state rule. Administrative readiness does not grant permission or credentials to an ordinary writer.

Available is point-in-time evidence only. Per-operation current authority, context pins, transaction liveness and required resource checks still apply. Dispose invalidates assembly readiness; drift invalidates every family whose required inventory is affected, not arbitrarily unrelated families. Unknown dependency impact conservatively makes dependent selections unavailable until coherent verification resolves it. Keep immutable qualification receipts distinct from current readiness observations; refresh never edits an old passing run into a new installation claim. Public reasons follow the host disclosure policy and omit hidden target details where required.

Proposed limits: 16 MiB receipt, 100,000 required cases/outcomes, 128 levels of JSON nesting and bounded host-resolved reference inventory. [evidence-receipt-v0.1.schema.json](evidence-receipt-v0.1.schema.json) now specifies the structural draft. Duration is exact nonnegative nanosecond decimal text. observedAt must pass semantic RFC 3339/time validation; schema string shape is not sufficient. Signature/trust integration requires binding review before implementation. Parse duplicate JSON keys exactly and refuse malformed content; no partial certification.

Independent tests must reject missing/duplicate cases, false pass with failed/skipped case, changed contract/model digest, wrong native target, forged runner trust and missing result artifact. Include a valid fixture receipt whose scope is structural only and prove it cannot satisfy native claims. US-026/028 and TP-001 own the corresponding implementation tests. This contract defines evidence meaning; it does not certify any current runtime profile.

## Structural schema obligations

A schema-valid receipt may truthfully contain failed/skipped/not_run cases and may come from an untrusted runner. Thus schema validity never establishes qualification. Semantic validation checks unique input/environment/case/outcome identities, exact required-case/outcome set equality, result artifacts for every claimed execution, required reason for skipped/blocked/not_run, actual pin/profile compatibility and host-established runner trust. Passed cases with no executable result evidence cannot certify a claim. A nonempty required manifest prevents vacuous structural success; broad claim completeness additionally requires the selected independent manifest, not a manifest supplied by the claimant.

## Explicit conformance tooling boundary

The [conformance declaration](bindings/truss-conformance-tooling-v0.1.d.ts) separates host runner invocation from evidence assessment. Importing/loading manifests and construction invoke no runner, database, process, network or artifact fetch. Host registers exact trusted runner/procedure/environment/resource profiles and resolves the environment reference through existing custody and current explicit test authority. Manifest/input bytes cannot nominate a command, executable module, credentials or trusted runner. Missing environment is unavailable, not a skipped full pass. Running the corpus is an explicit host operation; this design work does not authorize environment startup.

A recorded result preserves the exact existing CONTRACT-011 receipt, including every required case and honest failed/skipped/blocked/not_run state. Recorded is not qualified. Interrupted execution preserves original run identity/evidence and recovery reference; reconcile only that original run, never restart tests implicitly or issue a pass for missing outcomes. Cancellation cannot certify native cleanup. Host runner containment and owned-resource cleanup require their selected profile; no teardown removes unrelated host resources.

Assessment independently admits original receipt bytes/profiles/case inventory and exact selected required manifest, qualified host runner custody, actual target/implementation/source pins and complete result artifacts under this contract. Claimant-provided reduced manifests cannot replace the independent selected manifest. Qualification names its exact profile/manifest/receipt and assessment evidence; it proves only that scope. Resource/custody/authority failure is unavailable, malformed/missing/contradictory evidence is unqualified or integrity refusal, and neither becomes a pass. Concrete runner/environment/case manifest/assessment resource and construction profiles remain design gates; no existing structural probe becomes native support through this interface.

### Candidate migration ordinary-writer privilege closure

The protected migration profile must inventory five distinct principals: ordinary application caller, native procedure owner, integrity observer, authorized migration operator and deployment administrator. Native function execution must preserve separately captured actual acting role; procedure ownership and integrity visibility cannot supply administrative/disclosure permission. The operator invokes qualified migration/recovery entry points without gaining table ownership or direct protected-store DML. Deployment/guard changes invalidate the original installed inventory before further claims.

Ordinary callers have no direct UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE/DDL or identity/sequence-control privilege on schema_head, installation_admission, key_bucket_guard, bucket membership/reservation, migration receipt or complete-feed producer stores. Receipt/archive/private integrity SELECT is separately restricted. Ordinary graph read privileges remain governed by their selected policy/mapping profile; this rule cannot silently withdraw required SQL/read paths. Every callable helper, inherited role, PUBLIC/default grant and role-switch path must preserve the same closure. Direct-table writer support still requires its own independently qualified unavoidable derivation/registration profile.

There is a concrete head-admission privilege conflict to resolve: [PostgreSQL 17 SELECT documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-select.html) requires UPDATE privilege on at least one selected table column for row-locking clauses. Granting ordinary UPDATE on head revision merely to permit FOR SHARE can also permit caller head mutation. The protected candidate therefore invokes a narrowly qualified native head-admission routine on the caller's same transaction; its owner takes the share head lock and captures original caller/revision/configuration metadata. Ordinary callers receive EXECUTE only, with no direct head UPDATE. It retains the existing head-first hierarchy and fixed-snapshot retry behavior; no independent connection/advisory lock replaces the row lock. Exact routine identity/body/owner/search path and supported driver admission form are remaining design outputs, not a claimed installed function.

Native inventory must prove no caller-reachable definer helper accepts arbitrary protected-store writes, resets generation, rewrites receipt bytes, selects producing xid/clock or mutates head revision. Secure function namespace/settings and all reachable dependencies are explicit profile inputs. Attempt ordinary head mutation, role-switch/inherited grants, shadow routine/schema creation and helper argument abuse under the actual qualified caller. An observer-owned successful lock is not proof the ordinary EXECUTE-only path works. This closes required privilege distinctions and exposes the admission callable dependency for M-02/03; exact native definitions remain unresolved.


[Unapplied head-admission routine candidate](write-head-admission-v0.1.draft.sql) has no arguments, fully qualified relation access and an explicit restricted search path. It returns only head revision, native configuration generation and database-derived original acting-role text, keeping the share lock in the invoking transaction. Missing head/admission state or read-only scope refuses; no graph mutation or implicit transaction end occurs. There is no SQL/grant/role/payload input accepting arbitrary privileged operations. PUBLIC execution is revoked, and selected actual ordinary roles require an explicit qualified EXECUTE whitelist. Procedure owner is a separate controlled native role; no automatic owner-role membership is granted to callers.

These outputs are observation metadata, not an authorization token or full configuration/binding comparison. The writer still performs selected source/profile/policy/native admission and may not reuse them in another connection/transaction or after savepoint rollback releasing the lock. Actual same-transaction lock retention, definer role capture, temporary-name shadowing resistance, isolation retry/error mapping and owner privilege closure require native qualification. Early selection of an ordinary caller role does not establish all its owner grants. The routine is a candidate definition, not an installed dependency or a substitute for unavoidable writer guards.

Receipt enforcement support uses CONTRACT-009's per-surface privilege/assertion inventory. Native table/FK/check/guard/procedure evidence is scoped separately from engine canonical/source/result/owner admission. An ordinary-callable raw artifact writer cannot qualify original receipt/protection semantic integrity by virtue of SECURITY DEFINER or matching digest alone. Required effective role memberships, PUBLIC/default function execution, schema/dependency ownership and maintenance/restore paths are part of installed inventory; missing cases remain unverified. No authored DDL fragment or privileged host role implies database enforcement.

## Conformance resource admission and honest interruption

The selected runner/assessor resource profile must predeclare received manifest/inventory/receipt/artifact bytes, case/outcome/evidence counts, exact complete-envelope capacity, original archive/producer bounds, owned simultaneous memory and active execution/containment observation limits. Before any runner/environment effects, resolve and admit the complete required manifest and input inventory, reserve complete worst-case progress/receipt and original recovery evidence capacity, and reject unsupported producer ceilings. Case count alone or average outcome size cannot prove sufficient capacity. Complete per-case outcome manifests, failed/skipped/not_run outcomes and mandatory raw measurement evidence cannot be dropped, sampled or rewritten as digest-only evidence to fit. Larger qualified profiles may be selected explicitly before the run.

Runner resource refusal before acquiring native/environment resources is unavailable(resource), with no receipt claiming execution. After run effects begin, retain the original run identity/environment/progress and use interrupted with originalRunEvidence and original recovery reference whenever completion/cleanup cannot be established. A fully contained stopped run may return recorded only when the complete receipt truthfully includes every required case and actual failed/skipped/not_run outcome, raw evidence and original termination observations; recorded does not mean qualified. Unknown running cases cannot be relabeled not_run or failed from a timeout alone. The runner never emits a partial passing receipt or starts replacement work during reconciliation.

The assessor now explicitly returns unavailable(resource) when complete receipt/manifest/artifact admission or bounded complete assessment cannot finish under the selected profile. This is not unqualified-by-criteria failure or integrity corruption. Preserve those distinctions: false/contradictory original evidence is integrity; complete evidence proving failed/missing requirements yields unqualified; unavailable resources prove neither. No cached qualified verdict can replace the unfinished assessment, and no partial assessment can omit failing cases. Current-authority disclosure still applies to diagnostics and assessment evidence, while original protected run/evidence custody remains intact.

Independent scenarios fill exact manifest/outcome/artifact/byte limits, overflow before environment acquisition, interrupt active native work, lose cleanup replies, exhaust assessment artifact resolution and attempt to suppress raw performance samples. Verify no qualified verdict or recorded execution is manufactured, complete original evidence survives and reconciliation never launches a new run. Exact quantitative profiles, original producer/allocator/native containment and evidence-retention procedures remain design outputs before implementation qualification.

### Bounded reference conformance resource candidate

[truss-conformance-bounded-reference/0.1.0](bindings/conformance-resource-v0.1.candidate.json) proposes at most 1000 required cases, 4 MiB manifest, 16 MiB input inventory, 32 MiB complete receipt, 10000 complete archived evidence artifacts, 64 MiB per archived artifact, 1 GiB total archived evidence and 64 MiB owned assembly state. One receipt context is bounded to 1 MiB, each complete required-case entry to 4 KiB and each complete indexed case outcome to 16 KiB, including exact artifact references and diagnostics. Reserve context + requiredCaseCount × (perRequiredCaseEntry + perCaseOutcome) before runner effects: 1000 cases reserve 21528576 bytes, within the 33554432-byte receipt ceiling. This is conditional capacity arithmetic; actual lossless producer ceilings and complete reference inventory must be independently admitted. A case whose required outcome cannot fit refuses the selected profile before execution rather than truncating diagnostics.

Original raw observations/timing samples/mismatched outputs may reside in complete exact registered archive artifacts, referenced by the receipt under CONTRACT-011's custody rules. Archive references do not license digest-only replacement, omitted bytes, unavailable artifacts or failure suppression. Reserve exact worst-case required archive bytes/counts before native work; actual hidden/unbounded producer output cannot be justified by the receipt formula. Original recovery/progress/termination evidence and artifact indices count within selected complete producer/archive limits. Required larger evidence uses an explicitly selected larger qualified profile.

The candidate run deadline is one hour, assessment active time 30 seconds and containment observation 10 seconds. These are total selected procedure limits, not altered story performance thresholds, per-query native timeout claims or extra work during containment. A limit stop preserves true per-case outcomes and unresolved original work under the preceding interruption rules. Actual native server/environment/evidence-store resource controls remain separate qualification. All numerical limits are inclusive independent ceilings; receipt/manifest/inventory/raw/decoded simultaneous copies count against owned assembly bytes, so individual maxima need not fit together. Saturated new admission cannot evict original recovery. No native execution or RSS/storage-bound support follows from this candidate.

## Divergence observation and reviewed attribution

[Conformance divergence wire](conformance-divergence-v0.1.schema.json) separates immutable observed mismatch from subsequent reviewed attribution. Observed carries exact run/case/manifest/direction/surface, independent implementation/comparator pins, complete identity-alias/expected/both observed/native/comparison artifacts and unresolved attribution.
