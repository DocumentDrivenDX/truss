The directed source is Account and target is Item, resolved through its original `by-code` key. `targetMultiplicity` 0..2 permits zero, one or two Items per Account; `sourceMultiplicity` 0..1 permits zero or one Account per Item. These names describe opposite endpoint participation. These are owner source observations, not Truss native evidence.
