## Published release evidence

Admitted databases migrate forward without a release-age cutoff; previous application runtimes are not supported. ## What remains open

Broad unselected native discovery remains incomplete, and general performance budgets remain provisional. ABI1.2 indexes exact string/boolean equality and eligible single required-string-key pages for populations above10,000 rows. Complete candidate and byte limits still apply before residual filters and `LIMIT`; numeric, temporal, composite and larger-key routes use the bounded complete fallback. Complete-entity relationships remain capped at100 related keys. The retained million-object stress result exceeds the source cap for its full SQL list and does not establish unrestricted large-source queries. The public v0.0.48 migration and independent corpus checks cover only the linked source tuples and selected cases; larger database migration capacity and additional restore profiles need separate qualification.
