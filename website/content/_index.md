<section class="thesis" aria-label="Design principles"><div><span class="label">01 / Evolve the model</span><h3>Catalog rows.<br>Not migration churn.</h3><p>The storage design adds types, properties and relationships through catalog rows, without a table or index for every type.</p></div><div><span class="label">02 / Keep the meaning</span><h3>Every value<br>deserves its history.</h3><p>Properties and edges are the logical units of change. Exact values and unrecognized content have explicit preservation requirements.</p></div><div><span class="label">03 / Show the boundary</span><h3>Know which<br>rules actually hold.</h3><p>The intended enforcement report distinguishes database enforcement, engine enforcement and assertions neither enforces.</p></div></section>
